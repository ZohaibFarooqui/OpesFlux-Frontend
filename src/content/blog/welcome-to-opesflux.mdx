## Why we built OpesFlux

Most small businesses run on a till, a stock spreadsheet, an accounting package and a payroll service that don't talk to each other. Someone spends hours every week copying sales into the accounts and working out why the stock count doesn't match the till.

We built OpesFlux so that all of that is one system. A sale at the till takes stock off and posts to the accounts at the same moment. Goods received against a purchase order go straight into stock. Staff hours from the rota feed payroll.

OpesFlux is a product of Devs and Visuals, based in Kington, England. It is launching first in the UK and is already used by businesses in Pakistan and Japan.

## What OpesFlux does

- **Point of sale**: browser-based, barcode scanning, receipts, discounts, and it keeps selling offline
- **Stock control**: stock per location, transfers, low-stock alerts and a movement history that can't be edited
- **Purchasing**: purchase orders with approval, goods received notes and supplier payments
- **Sales and invoicing**: quotes, sales orders, invoices, recurring invoices, returns and credit notes, credit control
- **Accounts**: double-entry accounts, bank reconciliation with open banking feeds, VAT return figures
- **Staff and payroll**: rotas, attendance, leave, and UK payroll with PAYE, NI and pensions

Everything shares one database and one login.

## What it doesn't do (yet)

We'd rather you hear this from us. OpesFlux does not submit VAT returns or RTI to HMRC (your accountant or bridging software does that), and it has no loyalty schemes, gift cards, table service or batch tracking.
